Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a wig take to make?

Timelines depend on the style and length. We confirm your exact turnaround when we confirm your order.

How do I pay?

After you send your order, we confirm the total and share payment options. No payment is taken on this website.

Do you deliver?

We offer local pickup and delivery in the Houston and Dallas-Fort Worth areas. Delivery fees are confirmed with your order.

Can I choose a custom length or color?

Yes. Select options in the shop, or use the Custom Order form for something special.

How do I care for my braided wig?

Store it on a wig stand, keep it away from heat and tangles, and use a light moisturizing mist. We include care tips with every order.
